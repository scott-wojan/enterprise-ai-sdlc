## Implemented path

The prototype supports the complete representative path from public orientation to a readable leadership outcome. It includes structured responses, deterministic interpretation, hard-stop consequences, remediation verification, explicit scenario transition, complete-system evaluation, separate evidence and authority decisions, leadership investment choice, and a derived readable output.

- Proposed participation: Bounded Change

- Immediate result: Third active blocker
- Remediation: Verified for reassessment, without restored authority
- Later evidence conclusion: Negative net value demonstrated
- Later authority: Restricted to Assisted Work
- Leadership outcome: Fund one bounded remediation attempt

## Interaction behavior

- Save draft does not interpret or decide.
- Missing required response information creates a focused validation error.
- Interpretation can restrict but cannot approve.
- Remediation verification does not restore authority.
- Scenario transition explains elapsed organizational actions.
- Evidence conclusion, authority, and investment are recorded separately.
- The leadership output is derived and read-only.
