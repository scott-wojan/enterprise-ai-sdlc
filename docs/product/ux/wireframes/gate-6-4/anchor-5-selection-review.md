# Anchor 5 Selection Review

## Status

Desktop and mobile structures accepted on 12 August 2026 for the leadership investment decision moment. The desktop readable leadership output is also accepted. Its mobile adaptation remains pending.

These remain low-fidelity structural wireframes. Approval does not cover final typography, color, branding, component styling, detailed interaction behavior, prototype behavior or production implementation.

## Represented decision moment

The decision screen covers the later moment after:

- the completed-pilot evidence conclusion has been recorded as Negative net value demonstrated;
- the accountable authority decision has restricted the exact scope to Assisted Work;
- Bounded Change has stopped;
- expansion and standardization are unavailable;
- leadership has not yet recorded the investment disposition.

Leadership is not asked to reinterpret evidence or select AI authority.

## Decision-screen acceptance

On 12 August 2026, the product owner confirmed that the desktop screen makes the leadership decision and consequences clear without requiring leadership to reinterpret pilot evidence.

The product owner subsequently approved the mobile adaptation. The approval confirms that an executive can understand that the evidence and authority decisions are already settled, compare funding remediation against stopping investment, and recognize that recording the choice changes funding only.

## Accepted decision content order

1. initiative and completed-pilot context;
2. dominant investment question;
3. recommendation and non-expansion boundary;
4. recorded evidence and authority facts;
5. concise reason for the recommendation;
6. two mutually exclusive investment choices;
7. the commitment and consequence of each choice;
8. consequence of delaying the decision;
9. one action to record funding disposition;
10. explicit separation from evidence and authority;
11. decision owner and exact scope.

## Available investment choices

### Fund bounded remediation

Leadership may fund root-cause analysis and corrective work under the current Assisted Work boundary only when:

- accountable remediation owners are named;
- a cost ceiling is set;
- stop triggers are defined;
- another bounded evaluation is required.

Funding does not restore Bounded Change. Greater authority would require acceptable complete-system evidence and new approval.

### Stop further investment

Leadership may decline remediation and another evaluation for this use case. The organization closes the remediation path, preserves the evidence and decision record, and separately decides whether existing Assisted Work should also end.

## Unavailable choices

Leadership cannot select:

- continued or renewed Bounded Change;
- expansion;
- standardization;
- unrestricted continuation;
- an override of category restrictions;
- a different evidence conclusion.

## Decision separation

- The evidence conclusion is already recorded.
- The authority restriction is already recorded.
- Leadership records funding disposition only.
- The investment decision cannot change AI authority.
- The investment decision cannot override quality, service, security, finance or participant restrictions.
- The investment decision cannot alter the evidence conclusion.

## Readable-output represented moment

The selected output represents the later state after leadership recorded Fund bounded remediation.

The output is derived from the frozen evidence conclusion, recorded authority decision and recorded investment decision. It is a readable leadership brief, not a decision form or editable report.

## Desktop readable-output acceptance

On 12 August 2026, the product owner accepted the desktop structure for the recorded Fund bounded remediation outcome.

[Anchor 5 readable output desktop selected](explorations/anchor-5-readable-output-desktop-selected.png)

The accepted output makes these facts directly available:

1. leadership funded one bounded remediation attempt and did not approve expansion;
2. AI authority remains restricted to Assisted Work;
3. Bounded Change, expansion, standardization and favorable claims remain prohibited;
4. named remediation owners operate under a $25,000 cost ceiling and explicit stop triggers;
5. another bounded evaluation with complete-system evidence is required;
6. owners must submit a remediation plan for accountable review before corrective activity begins;
7. the brief cannot alter its source evidence, authority, restrictions or funding records.

## What these selections validate

- decision and recommendation before detail;
- one leadership job and one dominant action on the decision screen;
- two mutually exclusive choices;
- consequences before commitment;
- remediation as a capped learning investment rather than a guaranteed recovery;
- delay behavior;
- separation of evidence, authority and investment decisions;
- exact scope and accountable decision owner;
- preservation of the same decision meaning and option consequences on mobile;
- a readable post-decision outcome separated from decision entry;
- a visible accountable plan and recovery boundary;
- read-only derivation from source records.

## What these selections do not decide

- the mobile structure for the readable leadership output;
- detailed accessibility behavior;
- final visual design;
- working prototype or production implementation.
