# Anchor 4 Selection Review

## Status

Desktop structure accepted on 12 August 2026 for the completed-pilot evidence evaluation moment. Mobile structure remains pending.

This remains a low-fidelity structural wireframe. Approval does not cover final typography, color, branding, component styling, detailed interaction behavior, prototype behavior or production implementation.

## Represented moment

This review covers a separate later scenario in which:

- earlier readiness blockers were cleared through verified remediation and a new launch decision;
- the first controlled pilot completed;
- the evidence snapshot is frozen;
- the evidence is complete enough for a scoped conclusion;
- the formal evidence conclusion has not yet been recorded;
- the approved Bounded Change period ended;
- no expansion authority exists;
- authority and leadership investment decisions remain separate and unavailable from this screen.

## Rejected first attempt

The first desktop attempt was rejected because it exposed a dense evidence inventory instead of a usable evaluation experience.

[Rejected dense desktop attempt](explorations/anchor-4-desktop-rejected-dense.png)

The screen made the reader assemble the conclusion from:

- compact evidence rows;
- multiple states with similar hierarchy;
- claim restrictions;
- next actions;
- decision-separation notices.

The result, current decision status and route forward were not obvious.

## Product-owner acceptance

On 12 August 2026, the product owner confirmed that the simplified desktop screen communicates the five required takeaways:

1. Faster implementation did not create overall value.
2. The evidence is sufficient, but the accountable conclusion has not been recorded.
3. Favorable claims, Bounded Change renewal, expansion and standardization are unavailable.
4. The immediate action is to review and record the evidence conclusion.
5. Greater authority would require later remediation, reevaluation and approval.

## Selected artifact

[Anchor 4 desktop selected](explorations/anchor-4-desktop-selected.png)

## Accepted content order

1. restrained workspace and separate-scenario context;
2. dominant complete-system takeaway;
3. three supporting evidence facts;
4. current decision status;
5. immediate claim and authority restrictions;
6. one dominant action to review and record the evidence conclusion;
7. explicit separation from authority and investment decisions;
8. secondary recovery condition;
9. exact scope.

## Progressive-disclosure boundary

The primary screen does not reproduce the full evidence package. Detailed dimension results, limitations, source records, calculations, adverse evidence and independent dispositions remain available through the evidence review action.

The screen must not require a reader to reconstruct the complete-system takeaway from raw evidence.

## Decision separation

- The visible result is the evidence signal supported by the reviewed package.
- The formal evidence conclusion remains unrecorded until accountable action.
- Recording the evidence conclusion does not change AI authority.
- A separate authority decision determines permitted AI participation.
- A separate leadership investment decision determines whether remediation is funded or the use case stops.
- Verified remediation does not restore authority.

## What this selection validates

- result before process;
- complete-system priority over local implementation improvement;
- evidence sufficiency without implying a recorded conclusion;
- immediate claim and authority restrictions;
- one dominant next action;
- a findable longer recovery condition;
- separation of evidence, authority and investment decisions;
- removal of the dense evidence inventory from the primary path.

## What this selection does not decide

- the detailed evidence-review interaction;
- the authority-decision screen;
- the leadership investment decision;
- the readable leadership output;
- the mobile structure;
- detailed accessibility behavior;
- final visual design;
- working prototype or production implementation.

## Next validation target

Adapt the selected five-takeaway hierarchy to mobile. The mobile structure must preserve rapid comprehension without stacking so many explanatory sections that the result and next action become separated by a long scroll.
