# PROC-PILOT-005: Onboard a Pilot Team

## Purpose

Prepare each participant to use approved procedures, controls, support and stop routes on representative repository work before live pilot operation.

## Use when

PROC-PILOT-004 approves a pilot and before a participant receives live pilot authority.

## Do not use when

The charter, procedures, environment or participant role changed without review, or safe training and support are unavailable.

## Accountable owner

ROLE-OPS-015 pilot support and learning owner.

## Participants

- Pilot participants and their delivery owner.
- Platform, context, control, support and incident trainers.
- Review, service and product owners as applicable.

## Preconditions

- Approved ART-PILOT-001 and role-specific grant design.
- Representative safe training work and environment.
- Published procedures, support map and incident routes.

## Inputs

- Charter, participant role, eligible work and exclusions.
- Applicable Assisted Work or Bounded Change procedures.
- Control, evidence, stop, escalation and recovery scenarios.

## Actions

1. **The onboarding owner maps each role.** Identify allowed actions, prohibited actions, required artifacts, decisions and escalation.
2. **Participants learn the actual workflow.** Execute representative intake, context, evidence and procedure steps in a safe environment.
3. **Control owners demonstrate boundaries.** Show allowed and denied access, source handling, tool limits, checks, approval, expiry and revocation.
4. **Participants practice uncertainty and stop behavior.** Run missing-context, failed-check, sensitive-data, boundary, incident and recovery scenarios.
5. **Reviewers practice independent gates.** Distinguish AI suggestions, deterministic evidence and human approval.
6. **Participants use support routes.** Submit representative policy, context, technical and incident requests and confirm correct routing.
7. **The onboarding owner assesses readiness.** Use observable scenario outcomes, not attendance alone.
8. **Failed scenarios receive remediation and repeat.** Restrict or deny live authority until passed.
9. **The authority owner activates individual grants.** Bind participant, role, scope and expiry after passing evidence.

## Required human decisions

- The onboarding owner decides role preparation completeness.
- Control owners accept scenario results.
- The authority owner decides grant activation.
- Participants may decline or report unsafe conditions.

## Outputs

- Role-specific onboarding record and scenario evidence.
- Support-route test results.
- Remediation, restriction or passing decision.
- Activated or denied individual authority grant.

## Verification

- Participants can select the right procedure, preserve evidence and stop safely.
- Prohibited scenarios are denied.
- Support requests reach named owners.
- Training uses representative repository work.
- Attendance without demonstrated behavior does not pass.

## Stop conditions

Stop onboarding or deny authority when a participant cannot identify boundaries, apply controls, use evidence, stop, recover or reach support, or when training exposes unsafe incentives or system gaps.

## Completion criteria

- Every participant passes role-specific scenarios.
- Grants match demonstrated roles.
- Remediation and restrictions are recorded.
- PROC-PILOT-006 can operate with verified support.

## Measures

Record scenario failures, retraining, support-routing defects, time to preparedness, declined participation and post-onboarding procedure errors.

## Next procedures

- PROC-PILOT-006 for passing participants.
- PROC-PILOT-007 for recurring onboarding friction.

## Applicable principles and controls

- PRIN-001 through PRIN-012 and all applicable state, control and evidence requirements.
- ART-PILOT-001 and PROC-PILOT-004.
