# PROC-PILOT-008: Decide Continuation and Standardize Proven Practice

## Purpose

Turn a completed scorecard and failure inspection into a scoped continuation decision and standardize only practices supported by evidence for comparable risk.

## Use when

PROC-PILOT-003 and PROC-PILOT-007 are complete for a pilot review period.

## Do not use when

Mandatory guardrails fail, remediation is unverified, target scopes are materially different, or expansion is justified only by usage or anecdote.

## Accountable owner

ROLE-ENT-001 AI delivery governance owner.

## Participants

- Enterprise sponsor, value-stream, product, pilot and measurement owners.
- Platform, context, architecture, security, delivery, service and participant representatives.

## Preconditions

- Complete scorecard, ART-ADV-001 and inspection evidence.
- Known target scope and risk differences.
- Control and support capacity for any proposed continuation.

## Inputs

- PROC-PILOT-003 scorecard and decision evidence.
- PROC-PILOT-007 patterns and verified remediation.
- Readiness, control, outcome, cost and participant evidence.
- Proposed standard practice and target applicability.

## Actions

1. **Governance confirms the pilot decision.** Continue, condition, restrict, pause or stop the exact current scope.
2. **Proponents define any expansion precisely.** Name teams, work, repositories, states, risks, tools, authority and period.
3. **Owners compare applicability.** Identify organizational, context, architecture, data, risk and control differences from the evidence cohort.
4. **The method owner extracts candidate practice.** Separate invariant control intent from pilot-specific implementation and document prerequisites and limits.
5. **Control owners verify the standard.** Run allowed, prohibited, failure, revocation and recovery scenarios for the target class.
6. **Readiness owners assess each new scope.** No pilot result transfers readiness or authority automatically.
7. **Governance approves ART-ADV-001 for each exact scope.** State conditions, grant, owners, expiry and review.
8. **Platform and context owners publish the standard.** Version procedures, templates, training, support and migration while preserving risk overlays.
9. **The pilot owner closes or transitions the pilot.** Preserve evidence, revoke obsolete grants and communicate limitations.

## Required human decisions

- Governance decides continuation and each exact expansion.
- Domain and control owners decide target applicability.
- Enterprise sponsor decides investment in broader standardization.
- AI cannot generalize evidence into authority.

## Outputs

- Current-scope continuation or stop decision.
- Exact expansion decisions and grants, if any.
- Versioned standard practice with prerequisites, risk variations and exclusions.
- Adoption, training, support and review plan.
- Pilot closure and obsolete-authority revocation evidence.

## Verification

- Every decision is evidence-backed and scope-specific.
- New scopes pass their own readiness and controls.
- Standardization preserves higher-risk requirements.
- Usage does not substitute for outcomes.
- Obsolete grants and pilot-only infrastructure are retired or transferred explicitly.

## Stop conditions

Stop expansion on failed guardrails, inadequate evidence, unverified remediation, missing owners, non-comparable target risk, insufficient support or inability to implement exact technical restrictions.

## Completion criteria

- ART-ADV-001 decisions and technical state agree.
- Standard practice is versioned, validated and bounded.
- Owners and review cadence exist for each adopted scope.
- Pilot evidence and limitations remain discoverable.

## Measures

Record expansion scope, readiness failures, risk-specific deviations, standard adoption defects, support load, later restrictions and evidence claims corrected.

## Next procedures

- PROC-PILOT-004 for each new pilot scope.
- Standard operating procedures for approved continued scopes.
- Readiness reassessment or permanent stop.

## Applicable principles and controls

- PRIN-001 through PRIN-012 and all Pilot Edition state, readiness, control, evidence and measurement rules.
- ART-ADV-001 and the complete pilot scorecard.
