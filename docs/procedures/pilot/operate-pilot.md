# PROC-PILOT-006: Operate a Pilot

## Purpose

Run approved Assisted Work and Bounded Change procedures in normal delivery while preserving scope, support, evidence, guardrails and participant safety.

## Use when

The pilot is active and a passing participant submits eligible work.

## Do not use when

The charter expired, work is ineligible, authority or controls are stale, a stop is active, or the participant has not passed onboarding.

## Accountable owner

The pilot accountable owner.

## Participants

- Approved participants, change owners and reviewers.
- Support, control, measurement, service and incident owners.
- AI-SYS-001 within the applicable procedure and grant.

## Preconditions

- Active ART-PILOT-001, participant grant and passing control health.
- Eligible work intake and current procedure versions.
- Support, evidence and monitoring routes are operational.

## Inputs

- Work request and classification.
- Approved Assisted Work or Bounded Change procedure.
- Context, controls, artifacts, baseline and guardrails.

## Actions

1. **The participant checks eligibility.** Match work, state, USE, RISK, participant and repository to the charter.
2. **The participant selects the canonical procedure.** Do not invent a shortcut or combine authority from different procedures.
3. **Owners verify current inputs and controls.** Expired or mismatched evidence blocks the work.
4. **Participants execute the procedure exactly.** Preserve human decisions, deterministic gates, small batches and stop behavior.
5. **Support events become evidence.** Record question category, response, time, resolution, affected scope and systemic implication without surveilling individuals.
6. **Control and service owners monitor guardrails.** Invoke stop, revocation or incident response on triggers.
7. **The measurement owner captures fixed measures.** Preserve missing data and cohort membership.
8. **The pilot owner reviews operating evidence at cadence.** Correct route, context or support defects and restrict unsafe work immediately.

## Required human decisions

- Change and domain owners make procedure-specific consequential decisions.
- Control and incident owners decide containment.
- The pilot owner decides operating restriction within charter authority.
- AI cannot choose its procedure or expand eligible work.

## Outputs

- Procedure-specific artifacts and evidence.
- Work eligibility and disposition records.
- Support, friction, control, incident and guardrail evidence.
- Restrictions, remediations and current operating status.

## Verification

- Every AI-assisted event maps to eligible work, participant, procedure and grant.
- Required artifacts and gates are complete.
- Support events are retained and routed.
- Stops and restrictions alter technical authority.
- Measure definitions remain unchanged or a new baseline decision is made.

## Stop conditions

Stop affected work on ineligibility, stale authority, procedure bypass, control failure, incident trigger, guardrail breach, unsafe participant pressure, unhandled support gap or evidence loss.

## Completion criteria

- Each work item completes, stops or reroutes under its procedure.
- Evidence and support history are retained.
- Restrictions and incidents are implemented.
- Review-period data is ready for inspection and decision.

## Measures

Use approved pilot measures plus procedure adherence, support demand, stop frequency, control health and unclassified-work attempts.

## Next procedures

- PROC-PILOT-007 for failure and friction inspection.
- PROC-PILOT-003 at review or trigger.
- Incident and failure response immediately when required.

## Applicable principles and controls

- All Pilot Edition principles, controls, procedures and artifacts applicable to the selected workflow.
- ART-PILOT-001 and participant onboarding evidence.
