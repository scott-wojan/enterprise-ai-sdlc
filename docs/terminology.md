# Terminology

## Adoption state

A defined set of permitted AI actions, required capabilities, controls, human decisions and evidence. It is not a score of organizational sophistication.

## Agent

An AI-enabled system that can perform one or more actions using provided context and tools. The term does not imply unlimited autonomy.

## AI assistance

Use of an AI system to advise, analyze, draft or execute bounded software-delivery work under defined human accountability.

## Artifact

A durable, versioned input or output used to coordinate, govern or prove delivery work.

## Authority

The explicitly granted ability to access information, use tools, change state or initiate downstream activity.

## Bounded change

A change performed within an approved repository, branch, environment, scope and permission boundary, followed by required verification and human review.

## Consequential decision

A decision with material product, architectural, security, compliance, release, financial or operational impact.

## Context

Information made available to an AI system for a task, including specifications, code, architecture, policies, examples, operational data and tool results.

## Control

A preventive, detective or corrective mechanism that reduces the probability or impact of an undesirable outcome.

## Deterministic control

A control whose execution and result are reproducible, such as a test, policy rule, permission boundary or structural check.

## Evidence

A durable record showing that an action occurred, a requirement was evaluated or a decision was made.

## Governed workflow

A multi-step workflow in which AI may plan and execute bounded tasks between explicit validation and approval points.

## Harness

The collection of instructions, tools, tests, policies and feedback mechanisms that guides and constrains AI-assisted work.

## Human gate

A required decision made by a named accountable human role before work may continue.

## Inferential control

A probabilistic evaluation, often performed by an AI model, used where deterministic rules cannot fully evaluate meaning or intent.

## Selective orchestration

Approved coordination of multiple delivery activities or specialized agents for a bounded work type. It does not mean unrestricted autonomous delivery.

## Stop condition

A defined observation or event that requires work to halt, restrict authority or escalate to a human decision-maker.

## Verification

The act of producing evidence that specified requirements, controls and completion criteria have been satisfied.
