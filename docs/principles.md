# Governing Principles

## PRIN-001: Adopt by capability, not calendar

Teams expand AI use only when required capabilities exist and observed outcomes justify expansion.

## PRIN-002: Grant authority by work and risk

Authority is assigned to a bounded work type and environment. It is not granted merely because a tool supports greater autonomy.

## PRIN-003: Preserve human accountability

An accountable human role owns every consequential product, architecture, security, release and operational decision.

## PRIN-004: Require durable evidence

Material plans, approvals, actions and verification results must leave reviewable evidence.

## PRIN-005: Prefer deterministic enforcement

Tests, policies, isolation and executable checks take precedence over prompt instructions whenever a requirement can be enforced deterministically.

## PRIN-006: Work in small, reversible units

AI-assisted changes should be independently understandable, testable and reversible.

## PRIN-007: Measure the delivery system

Success is determined through balanced delivery, quality, operational, security, product, experience and economic outcomes. AI usage is not success by itself.

## PRIN-008: Apply least authority

Agents receive only the data, tools, permissions, time and environmental scope required for the approved task.

## PRIN-009: Manage context as an engineering asset

Specifications, instructions, architecture, procedures and operational knowledge must be versioned, owned, reviewed and improved.

## PRIN-010: Treat selective orchestration as optional

No team is required to pursue full lifecycle orchestration. The appropriate long-term state is the least autonomous state that achieves the desired outcomes safely and sustainably.

## PRIN-011: Improve the system after repeated failure

Repeated failures should result in improved context, workflows, tools or controls rather than repeated conversational correction.

## PRIN-012: Keep the core vendor-neutral

The normative playbook defines required behavior and evidence without depending on a specific product implementation.
