# ART-RISK-001: Change Risk Record

## Purpose

Preserve the human-approved classification that selects an eligible workflow and its required treatment.

## When required

For every proposed Bounded Change and every material change to its scope or impact.

## Owner

The human change owner with the classification owner.

## Required content

- Work, outcome, affected assets, data, environments and side effects.
- USE category, RISK class and state eligibility.
- Blast radius, reversibility, privilege, sensitivity, criticality, novelty and detectability assessment.
- Unknowns, assumptions, required domain decisions and highest applicable treatment.
- Required controls, boundary, checks, reviews, recovery and release conditions.
- Approvers, decision, reroute or denial and expiry.

## Producer and approver

The change owner produces it. Classification and required domain owners approve it. The executing AI cannot create the authoritative classification.

## Storage and retention expectations

Store with the work, plan, grant, verification and release evidence; preserve every version and reclassification.

## Consumers

PROC-BND-001 through PROC-BND-009, authority systems, reviewers and auditors.

## Validation

Every risk dimension is addressed; uncertainty never lowers risk; workflow matches USE and RISK; controls match treatment; approvals are authenticated and predate execution.

## Review or expiry conditions

Review on scope, data, dependency, environment, plan, side effect or incident change. Expire with the work or governing decision.
