# ART-ENT-001: Enterprise AI Delivery Charter

## Purpose

Authorize and bound the enterprise AI-assisted delivery program.

## When required

Before readiness assessment, evaluation or pilot activity and whenever stance or authority changes.

## Owner

ROLE-ENT-001, enterprise AI delivery governance owner.

## Required content

- Version, scope, objectives, exclusions and effective period.
- Named ROLE-ENT-001 through ROLE-ENT-004 owners and escalation routes.
- Approved AI stance, USE categories, state ceiling and non-exception boundaries.
- Decision rights, funding, support, incident, workforce and assurance commitments.
- Evidence, measurement, review, restriction and termination rules.
- Authenticated approval and technical implementation references.

## Producer and approver

The governance owner produces it with enterprise role owners. Executive risk and technology authorities approve it. AI may draft but cannot approve.

## Storage and retention expectations

Store in the governed enterprise system of record with version history, approvals, supersession and policy retention.

## Consumers

Readiness, classification, authority, pilot, control, audit and leadership processes.

## Validation

All roles resolve to people; scope and prohibitions are explicit; decisions align with policy; technical ceilings match; approval binds the exact version.

## Review or expiry conditions

Review at least annually and on material policy, leadership, incident, tool, state or risk change. Expire on the stated date or supersession.
