# ART-PILOT-001: Pilot Charter

## Purpose

Authorize one bounded pilot and make its outcomes, conditions, owners and stop rules executable.

## When required

Before participant onboarding or live Assisted Work or Bounded Change.

## Owner

The pilot accountable owner.

## Required content

- Exact team, repository, work, state, USE, RISK, identities, tools and period.
- Intended outcomes, baseline version, guardrails and diagnostic measures.
- Included and excluded participants, work, data, environments and actions.
- Control profile, authority grants and known limitations.
- Support, incident, escalation, recovery and participant-safety routes.
- Launch, pause, rollback, stop, completion and review criteria.
- Named owners, approvals and technical-state confirmation.

## Producer and approver

The pilot owner produces it with delivery, measurement and control owners. AI delivery governance and required domain owners approve it.

## Storage and retention expectations

Store with baseline, readiness, control and review evidence for the pilot retention period.

## Consumers

All pilot participants, support and incident owners, delivery procedures and evaluation decisions.

## Validation

Scope matches readiness and grants; measures reference the baseline; every stop has an owner and action; support and incident routes are tested; approvals bind the version.

## Review or expiry conditions

Review at the charter cadence and every scope, authority, control, incident or guardrail trigger. Expire automatically at the end date.
