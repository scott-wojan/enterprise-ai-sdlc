# ART-ADV-001: Adoption Advancement Decision

## Purpose

Record an evidence-backed decision to continue, condition, expand, restrict, pause or stop an exact AI-assisted delivery scope.

## When required

At pilot review, state transition, authority expansion, restoration or termination.

## Owner

The AI delivery governance owner.

## Required content

- Exact current and proposed team, work, state, risk, authority and period.
- Baseline and balanced flow, quality, risk and experience evidence.
- Control effectiveness, incidents, exceptions, failures and remediations.
- Participant and affected-party evidence, confounders and limitations.
- Alternatives including no change, restriction and stop.
- Decision, rationale, exact grant or restriction, owners, conditions and next review.

## Producer and approver

Pilot and measurement owners assemble evidence. Governance and required consequential owners approve. AI may summarize but cannot decide advancement.

## Storage and retention expectations

Store with readiness, pilot, control and prior advancement history under protected evidence controls.

## Consumers

State and grant systems, pilot teams, control owners, leadership, audit and future readiness assessment.

## Validation

Decision is not inferred from usage; balanced outcomes and adverse evidence are present; claims match evidence quality; scope and authority are exact; technical state matches.

## Review or expiry conditions

Review at the stated date and on guardrail, incident, scope, control or evidence change. Expire with its grant or superseding decision.
