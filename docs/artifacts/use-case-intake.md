# ART-USE-001: AI Delivery Use-Case Intake

## Purpose

Capture enough evidence to classify, assess and accept or reject a proposed AI-assisted delivery use.

## When required

Before readiness or pilot selection for every new or materially changed use case.

## Owner

The human use-case sponsor.

## Required content

- Identifier, sponsor, team, repositories, work types and intended outcome.
- Users, affected parties, data, tools, environments, destinations and side effects.
- Existing process and baseline evidence.
- Proposed state and authority, exclusions and alternatives including no AI use.
- Initial USE and RISK assessment, consequential owners and policy constraints.
- Acceptance evidence, guardrails, support, incident and recovery needs.
- Human intake decision and next action.

## Producer and approver

The sponsor produces it with delivery and domain owners. The intake or governance owner approves routing. AI may organize supplied facts only.

## Storage and retention expectations

Store with classification, readiness and pilot records under applicable evidence and data-handling rules.

## Consumers

PROC-READY-001, PROC-PILOT-001, PROC-PILOT-002 and PROC-BND-001.

## Validation

Scope is specific; owners and affected parties are named; data and side effects are disclosed; baseline and acceptance are observable; unknowns are explicit; no authority is implied by submission.

## Review or expiry conditions

Review on scope, owner, work, data, tool, environment, state, risk or policy change. Expire when rejected, withdrawn or superseded.
