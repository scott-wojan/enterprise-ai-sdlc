# ART-VERIFY-001: Verification Record

## Purpose

Preserve independent deterministic results and accountable human-review evidence for one exact candidate.

## When required

For every Bounded Change candidate and other work with required controls.

## Owner

The human verification owner.

## Required content

- Candidate, base, plan, boundary and environment identities.
- Expected control set and each control version, input, raw evidence and outcome.
- Scope comparison, failures, unable-to-evaluate results and limitations.
- Human review focus, findings, responses, reviewers and dispositions.
- Separate deterministic and human gate results.
- Overall eligible or blocked decision and downstream reference.

## Producer and approver

Trusted control systems produce results. Verification and review owners approve their distinct gates. AI cannot approve either.

## Storage and retention expectations

Store immutably outside executor control with the exact candidate and release evidence.

## Consumers

Release, failure response, audit, measurement and advancement decisions.

## Validation

Every required control appears; failures remain visible; results match the exact candidate; reviewer identities are valid; candidate drift invalidates the record.

## Review or expiry conditions

Expire on candidate, check, environment, requirement or risk change and at any policy-defined age limit.
