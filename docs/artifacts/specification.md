# ART-SPEC-001: Delivery Specification

## Purpose

State human-approved intent and observable acceptance independently from the implementation plan.

## When required

Before Bounded Change planning and whenever intended behavior changes.

## Owner

The human product or requirements owner.

## Required content

- Objective, actors, scope, exclusions and source versions.
- Required behavior, constraints, interfaces, data and failure handling.
- Observable acceptance examples and nonfunctional expectations.
- Consequential decisions, owners and dispositions.
- Assumptions, unknowns, conflicts and unresolved risks.
- Approval, effective version and trace links to work and verification.

## Producer and approver

The requirements owner produces and approves intent with domain owners. AI may analyze and draft but cannot decide intent.

## Storage and retention expectations

Version with the work and preserve superseded intent through completion and required audit retention.

## Consumers

Planning, execution, verification, review, release and examples.

## Validation

Acceptance is observable; intent is distinct from implementation steps; sources and decisions resolve; unknowns are owned; approval binds the version.

## Review or expiry conditions

Review on requirement, constraint, risk, interface or acceptance change. Supersession invalidates dependent unexecuted plans.
