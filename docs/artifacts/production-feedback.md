# ART-FEEDBACK-001: Production Feedback Record

## Purpose

Capture operational, user, quality and risk evidence after release without converting anecdote or usage into success.

## When required

During the defined observation period for a released pilot change or Assisted Work outcome used downstream.

## Owner

The human service or outcome owner.

## Required content

- Scope, release or use reference, observation period and data lineage.
- Intended outcomes, baseline, guardrails and diagnostic measures.
- Operational behavior, defects, incidents, support, rollback and user evidence.
- Participant and affected-party feedback, including adverse evidence.
- Confounders, limitations, facts, inference and unresolved risks.
- Human disposition and actions.

## Producer and approver

Operational systems and authorized participants produce evidence. Service, measurement and risk owners approve interpretation.

## Storage and retention expectations

Store with release and pilot evaluation evidence using appropriate privacy, workforce and operational access rules.

## Consumers

Evaluation, incident, failure inspection, control improvement and advancement decisions.

## Validation

Evidence links to exact scope and period; measures retain definitions; adverse results remain visible; causal claims state limits; usage is not labeled value.

## Review or expiry conditions

Review at pilot cadence and on incidents, guardrail breaches or material feedback. Close after the observation period but retain history.
