# Team Onboarding Workshop

## Purpose

Have participants demonstrate, on representative safe work, that they can follow the applicable procedures, recognize limits, preserve evidence, use support and stop safely.

## Required outputs

- Role-to-procedure map for each participant.
- Completed allowed, denied, uncertainty, failure, support and incident scenarios.
- Participant-specific pass, remediate, restrict or deny decision.
- Individual authority activation evidence where passed.

## Participants

Pilot participants, their delivery owner, onboarding owner, qualified reviewer, platform and context support, control owner and incident representative.

## Pre-work

- Active ART-PILOT-001 and approved training environment.
- Representative repository exercise with no production or customer data.
- Role-specific procedures, artifacts, grant design and prohibited actions.
- Working support and incident routes.
- Scenario results expected from deterministic controls.

## Agenda

1. **Explain role and authority, 15 minutes.** Participants restate permitted work and prohibitions.
2. **Choose a procedure, 15 minutes.** Given sample requests, participants select Assisted Work, Bounded Change, human-only or stop.
3. **Run the normal scenario, 30 minutes.** Produce required artifacts and evidence.
4. **Run uncertainty and missing-context scenarios, 20 minutes.** Participants must stop and escalate.
5. **Run denied-action and failed-control scenarios, 25 minutes.** Verify technical denial and no silent waiver.
6. **Run support and incident routes, 20 minutes.** Submit representative requests and invoke revocation.
7. **Debrief and decide, 15 minutes.** Record friction, remediation and individual status.

## Stop conditions

Deny live authority when a participant cannot identify procedure, facts versus inference, human decisions, evidence, deterministic failures, prohibited data, boundary breach or stop and incident routes. Stop the workshop if the environment or support route behaves unsafely.

## Follow-up

Use PROC-PILOT-005 for formal records and grant activation. Failed scenarios receive owned remediation and repeat. Workshop attendance never substitutes for passing evidence.
