# Pilot Design Workshop

## Purpose

Produce an approvable ART-PILOT-001 with representative bounded work, baseline, guardrails, support, incident, recovery and stop criteria.

## Required outputs

- Selected or rejected team and use case.
- Completed ART-PILOT-001 and linked ART-USE-001, ART-READY-001 and baseline.
- Exact control profile, participant scope, support map and authority design.
- Launch, pause, rollback, stop, completion and review criteria.

## Participants

Pilot, value-stream, product, governance and measurement decision owners must attend. Include team representatives, platform, context, control, security, service, support, incident and recovery owners.

## Pre-work

- Complete candidate intake, readiness and baseline evidence.
- Supply at least one representative work sample and explain why it is not showcase work.
- Draft USE and RISK, eligible procedures, participants, controls and exclusions.
- Rehearse control, support, incident and recovery routes or list failed evidence.
- Pre-populate ART-PILOT-001 and circulate decisions needed.

## Agenda

1. **Confirm candidate and willingness, 15 minutes.** Verify ownership, capacity and safe participation.
2. **Test representativeness and boundary, 20 minutes.** Decide work, repository, data, state, risk and exclusions.
3. **Review baseline and outcomes, 25 minutes.** Fix balanced measures, guardrails, lineage and claim limitations.
4. **Review control and recovery evidence, 25 minutes.** Resolve failed allowed, denied, stop, incident or recovery scenarios.
5. **Design operation, 20 minutes.** Set onboarding, support, work intake, review cadence and evidence routes.
6. **Set stop and completion rules, 20 minutes.** Make triggers observable and assign action and authority owners.
7. **Decide launch, 15 minutes.** Approve, condition, return for remediation or reject.

## Stop conditions

Do not launch with missing baseline, coerced participation, showcase-only work, denied or excessive-risk work, untested controls, inadequate reviewers, unresolved recovery, vague stop criteria or mismatched technical authority.

## Follow-up

Invoke PROC-PILOT-004. Technical owners implement the approved charter and independent verification confirms the match. Only then may PROC-PILOT-005 begin.
