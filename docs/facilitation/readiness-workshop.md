# Readiness Decision Workshop

## Purpose

Turn completed readiness evidence into ART-READY-001, exact state and authority restrictions, and owned remediation. The workshop reviews evidence; it does not discover readiness from opinions in the room.

## Required outputs

- One finding for every applicable CTRL-READY control.
- ART-READY-001 with selected state, eligible and prohibited work and exact authority effect.
- Remediation owners, actions, evidence and reassessment triggers.
- Authenticated approve, restrict, remain in Foundation or no-use decision.

## Participants

AI delivery governance and delivery accountable owners must attend. Include the organizational, team, engineering, context, control and measurement condition owners and required risk, product, service and participant representatives.

## Pre-work

- Complete the readiness evidence collection under PROC-READY-001.
- Resolve exact enterprise, team, repository, work, data, environment and identity scope.
- Provide evidence links, versions, finding proposals and limitations for every condition.
- Circulate the draft ART-READY-001 and list all missing or conflicting evidence.
- Identify state alternatives and the authority effect of every gap.

Do not schedule a decision workshop while material condition evidence is absent.

## Agenda

1. **Confirm scope and decision owner, 10 minutes.** Reject unresolved overlap or hidden scope.
2. **Review organizational and team evidence, 20 minutes.** Decide each finding and record participant-safety concerns.
3. **Review engineering and context evidence, 25 minutes.** Test repository health, instructions, provenance and recovery evidence.
4. **Review control and measurement evidence, 25 minutes.** Confirm allowed, denied and unable-to-evaluate behavior plus baseline feasibility.
5. **Apply the least-capable state rule, 20 minutes.** Select the least authority needed for eligible work and prohibit everything else.
6. **Convert gaps into restrictions, 20 minutes.** Name remediation, owner, evidence and reassessment trigger.
7. **Authenticate the decision, 10 minutes.** Approve ART-READY-001 and assign technical implementation.

## Evidence questions

- Does the evidence cover the exact candidate scope and current version?
- What happens technically when this control fails or cannot evaluate?
- Can stop, revocation, recovery and evidence preservation work without AI cooperation?
- Does every gap alter actual authority?
- Can a willing participant reach support and report harm safely?

## Stop conditions

Stop when evidence is stale, condition ownership is absent, composite scoring replaces findings, gaps have no authority effect, the desired state was chosen before review, participant concerns are suppressed or technical systems cannot implement the decision.

## Follow-up

The governance owner verifies technical state against ART-READY-001. Remediation owners provide observable completion evidence. Reassessment uses PROC-READY-001 and creates a new artifact version; the workshop does not silently reopen the decision.
