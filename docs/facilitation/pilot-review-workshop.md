# Pilot Review Workshop

## Purpose

Produce an evidence-backed continue, condition, expand, restrict, pause or stop decision and, where justified, a risk-preserving standardization proposal.

## Required outputs

- Completed pilot decision scorecard.
- Dimension-level findings, guardrail status, confounders and claim limits.
- ART-ADV-001 with exact scope, authority, conditions and owners.
- Failure-inspection actions and any bounded standardization candidate.

## Participants

AI delivery governance, pilot, measurement, product, value-stream, control, service and participant decision owners must attend. Include platform, context, architecture, security, workforce and finance owners when their dimensions are material.

## Pre-work

- Freeze the dataset and complete the scorecard under PROC-PILOT-003.
- Complete PROC-PILOT-007 for failures and recurring friction.
- Circulate all dimension results, adverse evidence, participant feedback, costs, missing data and confounders.
- Draft decision alternatives, exact scope changes and technical authority effects.
- Verify remediation evidence for any proposed restoration or expansion.

## Agenda

1. **Confirm scope and evidence fitness, 15 minutes.** Default to restriction on material unreliability.
2. **Review guardrails, 25 minutes.** Quality, control, operational and participant-safety failures are considered before benefits.
3. **Review flow and product outcomes, 20 minutes.** State practical significance and causal limits.
4. **Review experience and economics, 20 minutes.** Include review, support, control, remediation and hidden effort.
5. **Review failure and friction patterns, 20 minutes.** Confirm owned system changes and restrictions.
6. **Consider alternatives, 20 minutes.** Continue, condition, exact expansion, restrict, pause or stop without a composite score.
7. **Decide standardization, 15 minutes.** Separate transferable control intent from pilot-specific implementation and preserve risk differences.
8. **Authenticate and implement, 15 minutes.** Complete ART-ADV-001 and assign technical changes and verification.

## Stop conditions

Do not expand when any mandatory guardrail fails, adverse evidence is omitted, participant reporting is unsafe, remediation is unverified, target scope differs materially, owners or support are absent, or usage is presented as value.

## Follow-up

Invoke PROC-PILOT-008. Verify grants and controls match ART-ADV-001, publish bounded lessons, retire obsolete authority and schedule the next review. New scopes begin with their own readiness and PROC-PILOT-004.
