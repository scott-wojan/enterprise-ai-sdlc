# Leadership Alignment Session

## Purpose

Produce an approvable ART-ENT-001, named enterprise roles and a bounded enterprise AI stance. This is a decision session, not an awareness presentation.

## Required outputs

- Draft or approved ART-ENT-001.
- Named ROLE-ENT and ROLE-OPS-001 through ROLE-OPS-004 owners.
- Enterprise outcomes, exclusions, state ceiling and non-exception boundaries.
- Decision, evidence, funding, workforce, support and incident commitments.
- Owned gaps and a date for any unresolved approval.

## Participants

The enterprise sponsor and AI delivery governance decision owner must attend. Include engineering policy, technology, product, risk, security, legal or compliance, workforce, finance and value-stream leaders needed to decide their domains. Observers cannot substitute for decision owners.

## Pre-work

Distribute at least three working days before the session:

- Current enterprise policy and AI initiatives.
- Candidate outcomes and affected value streams.
- Known data, security, regulatory, workforce and vendor constraints.
- Draft role assignments and decision-right conflicts.
- ART-ENT-001 template with known evidence already entered.
- The Foundation, Assisted Work and Bounded Change state definitions.

The facilitator returns incomplete pre-work. Live opinion must not replace unavailable evidence.

## Agenda

1. **Confirm decisions and authority, 10 minutes.** State who can approve the charter and what remains out of scope.
2. **Agree on outcomes and alternatives, 25 minutes.** Define why the enterprise is acting, observable intended outcomes and the no-AI alternative.
3. **Set the state ceiling and prohibitions, 25 minutes.** Decide whether Foundation, Assisted Work or Bounded Change may be considered and name denied work and non-exception boundaries.
4. **Assign accountability, 25 minutes.** Resolve each enterprise role to one named human, delegate rule and escalation path.
5. **Commit the operating foundation, 30 minutes.** Decide funding, participant protection, support, incident, evidence, measurement and review commitments.
6. **Review the charter, 25 minutes.** Read each material ART-ENT-001 section, record approve, revise or reject and identify technical enforcement.
7. **Close decisions, 10 minutes.** Authenticate decisions, owners, dates and stop conditions.

## Facilitation prompts

- Which outcome would justify the learning cost without assuming AI adoption is the answer?
- What work remains prohibited even if a vendor supports it?
- Who can stop the program and how quickly can authority be removed?
- What evidence would cause leadership to restrict or end the pilot?
- How will participants report failure or pressure safely?

## Decision capture

Record decisions directly in ART-ENT-001 and ART-APPROVAL-001 with exact version, approver, evidence, alternatives, rationale, conditions and expiry. Record disagreement rather than averaging it. The most restrictive applicable decision governs until resolved.

## Stop conditions

End without approval when the sponsor or governance owner is absent, prohibited boundaries are disputed, accountability remains shared ambiguously, participant protections are rejected, required policy evidence is unavailable or technical authority cannot implement the stance.

## Follow-up

The governance owner publishes the approved charter through the authorized route, verifies technical ceilings and sends owned gaps to readiness. The facilitator checks completion at the recorded date. No evaluation or pilot begins from meeting notes alone.
