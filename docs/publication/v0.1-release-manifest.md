# Version 0.1 Pilot Edition Release Manifest

## Release identity

- **Release:** Enterprise AI-Assisted Software Delivery Playbook, version 0.1 Pilot Edition.
- **Status:** Pilot-ready, vendor-neutral core guidance.
- **Release date:** 2026-08-09.
- **Release boundary:** Foundation, Assisted Work and Bounded Change only.
- **Primary navigation:** [Pilot Edition information architecture](pilot-edition-information-architecture.md).

Pilot-ready means a qualified enterprise team can use repository content alone to design, run, stop and evaluate a bounded pilot. It does not mean the playbook has been validated across every enterprise, platform, jurisdiction or delivery domain.

## Included capabilities

### Normative foundation

- Human accountability and consequential decisions.
- Bounded authority, risk, durable evidence and control precedence.
- Small batches, reversibility, context ownership, exceptions and system learning.
- Foundation, Assisted Work and Bounded Change state definitions and decisions.

### Readiness and enterprise foundation

- Organizational, team, engineering, context, control and measurement readiness.
- Executable readiness decision and remediation.
- Enterprise roles, AI stance, approved-use categories, risk classes and minimum control baseline.
- Baseline establishment and evidence-based pilot selection.

### Executable delivery

- Seven Assisted Work procedures with read-only or advisory authority.
- Nine Bounded Change procedures from classification through release or failure response.
- Integrated pilot start, onboarding, operation, inspection, evaluation and standardization.

### Operational substrate

- Identity, authorization, isolation, context, tool, verification, approval, audit, evidence, incident and revocation controls.
- Fifteen accountable human operating roles plus an explicitly non-accountable AI actor.
- Formula-backed balanced outcome measures and a non-composite decision scorecard.

### Adoption assets

- Twelve artifact definitions and matching fill-in templates.
- Complete Assisted Work and Bounded Defect Correction examples.
- Five decision-producing facilitation guides.
- Traceability, validation and source governance.

## Required consumption sequence

1. Approve enterprise ownership and stance.
2. Complete readiness and select the least-capable eligible state.
3. Establish the baseline and pilot charter with stop criteria.
4. Implement and rehearse the minimum control profile.
5. Onboard participants through demonstrated scenarios.
6. Operate only the canonical procedure matching approved work.
7. Inspect failure and improve system elements.
8. Evaluate balanced outcomes and make an exact human decision.
9. Standardize only evidence-supported practice for comparable risk.

## Release validation

The release is accepted only when:

- Repository content validation passes.
- Seeded negative validation tests pass.
- Every Pilot Edition backlog issue from #20 through #74 is closed by merged work.
- Audience entry points and relative links are discoverable.
- Procedures include human decisions, verification, stop and completion criteria.
- Controls and artifacts satisfy their schemas.
- Worked examples follow the normative flow and show stop paths.
- No required path depends on a named commercial platform.

## Known limitations

- The core release does not provide product-specific setup commands, configurations or reference implementations.
- Measures require each enterprise to supply local systems of record, cohort definitions and pre-pilot data.
- Regulatory, employment, privacy, records and security obligations must be reconciled with applicable enterprise and jurisdictional requirements.
- The examples are fictional and demonstrate method, not expected performance.
- The release has repository validation and internal consistency checks but not broad external field validation.
- Markdown is canonical. A responsive publication site and generated distribution formats are not included in version 0.1.

## Explicitly deferred capabilities

- Governed multi-step workflows beyond Pilot Edition Bounded Change.
- Selective orchestration and inter-agent delegation.
- Mixed-state portfolio and enterprise-scale operating governance.
- Platform-specific adapters and control mappings.
- Machine-readable traceability after identifiers and field use stabilize.
- Version 1.0 cross-enterprise validation and reconciliation.

These capabilities are described in the [roadmap](../roadmap.md). Their absence must not be filled by silently expanding Pilot Edition authority.

## Change and release control

Changes to normative content require the repository's issue, review, validation and decision processes. A material boundary or architecture change requires a recorded methodology decision. A future version must preserve this manifest, identify superseded content and state migration and compatibility implications.
