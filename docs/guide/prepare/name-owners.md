# Name the Pilot Owner and Decision Group

## Why am I here?

Turn general support for a pilot into named human accountability before selecting work or configuring tools.

**Owner:** Executive sponsor  
**Participants:** Pilot lead, engineering, product, platform, security or risk, verification, release and measurement owners  
**Time:** 45 minutes  
**Bring:** The pilot intent and required accountabilities  
**Leave with:** A named pilot lead, decision group, decision rights and escalation path

## Do this

1. Name the executive sponsor and the pilot lead.
2. Name one person for each accountability described in [Who must participate](../understand/who-participates.md).
3. State which decisions each person owns.
4. Identify decisions that require more than one independent person.
5. Name a backup for time-critical decisions.
6. Agree who may pause the pilot and who may restart it.
7. Record the normal escalation path and the urgent incident path.
8. Confirm the time each participant will actually provide.

## Decisions that need explicit owners

At minimum, assign ownership for:

- approving the pilot intent and resources;
- accepting or rejecting a use case;
- deciding readiness and required remediation;
- approving access, tools and boundaries;
- accepting work into the delivery and release process;
- responding to security, safety or operational events;
- protecting measurement integrity;
- deciding whether to continue, restrict, expand or stop.

AI may support these decisions but cannot own or approve them.

## Check the result

Ask each named person to explain their decision rights and when they must stop or escalate. Resolve overlaps and gaps now. A role list is incomplete if two people each believe the other owns a decision.

## Stop here if

- The sponsor delegates the final enterprise decision without naming an accountable replacement.
- The pilot lead cannot pause work.
- Someone can approve their own consequential action without independent review.
- A required participant is only expected to join after launch.

Record the result in the [Pilot Workbook](../reference/pilot-workbook.md). Detailed role definitions remain in the [canonical role model](../../roles/enterprise-and-value-stream.md).
