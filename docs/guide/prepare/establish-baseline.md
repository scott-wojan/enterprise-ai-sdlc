# Establish the Baseline and Success Measures

## Why am I here?

Create a fair comparison before AI-assisted work begins so the final decision is based on evidence instead of impressions.

**Owner:** Measurement owner  
**Participants:** Pilot lead, engineering manager, product owner, delivery team, quality, operations and risk partners  
**Time:** 60 to 90 minutes plus data collection  
**Bring:** Recent comparable work and available delivery, quality, operations and cost data  
**Leave with:** A baseline, measure definitions, data owners and review cadence

## Measure a balanced set

Choose a small set that the organization can collect consistently:

- **Flow:** elapsed time, waiting, rework and throughput for comparable work.
- **Quality:** escaped defects, review findings, test results and acceptance failures.
- **Operations:** change failures, incidents, recovery work and support burden.
- **Security and control:** denied actions, exceptions, evidence gaps and policy events.
- **Product:** acceptance, usefulness and whether the intended outcome was achieved.
- **Team experience:** confidence, cognitive load, frustration, learning and willingness to continue.
- **Economics:** tool cost, additional review, remediation, support and enablement effort.

Usage volume, generated code and model-reported success are diagnostics, not proof of value.

## Define each measure

For each selected measure, write:

- the question it helps answer;
- the precise definition;
- source and owner;
- collection frequency;
- comparison group or period;
- known limitations;
- the condition that would trigger investigation or a stop.

Preserve the raw evidence needed to explain the result. Do not reduce the pilot to one composite score.

## Protect the comparison

Use comparable work wherever possible. Note changes in staffing, release cadence, product scope or incident load. Collect the baseline before onboarding effects are mixed into the result.

## Stop here if

The baseline cannot distinguish delivery improvement from increased risk, hidden rework or transferred effort. Remediate the measurement design or narrow the pilot.

Record the baseline in the [Pilot Workbook](../reference/pilot-workbook.md). Detailed definitions are in [baseline, flow and quality measures](../../measures/baseline-flow-quality.md) and [balanced outcomes](../../measures/balanced-outcomes.md).
