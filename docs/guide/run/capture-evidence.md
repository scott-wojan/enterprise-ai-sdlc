# Capture Evidence Without Burdening the Team

## Why am I here?

Collect enough trustworthy evidence to make decisions without turning the pilot into a reporting project.

**Owner:** Measurement owner  
**Participants:** Pilot lead, delivery team, engineering manager, product, platform, quality, operations and risk partners  
**Time:** A few minutes per material event and 15 minutes before the weekly review  
**Bring:** Baseline definitions, normal delivery data and the current workbook  
**Leave with:** Decision-ready evidence that can be traced to real work

## Capture by exception

Use normal system data wherever it is trustworthy. Ask the team to record:

- a meaningful improvement or unexpectedly useful result;
- a material error, omission or misleading output;
- significant rework or transferred effort;
- a denied action, exception, near miss or incident;
- a repeated context or workflow problem;
- a material change in confidence, cognitive load or support need;
- a cost that the normal delivery data does not show.

Do not require a transcript of every interaction. Preserve exact prompts or outputs only when they are necessary to reconstruct a consequential decision, failure or approval and may be stored safely.

## Keep evidence usable

For each material observation, record:

- the work item and date;
- what happened;
- the operating mode and applicable charter version;
- observable impact;
- supporting source or link;
- who assessed it;
- uncertainty or limitation;
- action or decision.

Separate facts from interpretation. AI self-reports, generated summaries and confident language are not independent evidence.

## Protect people and information

Do not use measurement to rank individual developers. Remove secrets and unnecessary personal information. Restrict incident and sensitive evidence to the appropriate protected system.

## Check burden weekly

If evidence capture routinely takes longer than the work being observed, simplify it. If important failures cannot be reconstructed, strengthen it. The correct level supports decisions without changing team behavior merely to satisfy reporting.

## Stop here if

Evidence is being fabricated, backfilled from memory, used to punish honest reporting, or stored where sensitive content is exposed.
