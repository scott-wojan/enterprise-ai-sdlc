# Launch and Onboard the Team

## Why am I here?

Confirm that the approved pilot can operate in practice and that every participant knows what to do before real work begins.

**Owner:** Pilot lead  
**Participants:** Delivery team, engineering manager, product, platform, security or risk, verification, release and support owners  
**Time:** 60 to 90 minutes  
**Bring:** Approved charter, tested boundary, team roster, support routes and baseline  
**Leave with:** An onboarded team and an explicit start, delay or stop decision

## Before the session

The pilot lead confirms that:

- the exact charter version is approved;
- readiness remediation required before launch is closed;
- identities, access, isolation, logging, revocation and recovery have been tested;
- ordinary build, test, review and release checks pass;
- the baseline is recorded;
- support and incident responders are available;
- the first work item is eligible and ready.

## Onboard the team

1. Explain the pilot intent and what decision it will inform.
2. Show the selected use cases and explicit exclusions.
3. Explain Assisted Work and, if approved, Bounded Change in plain language.
4. Demonstrate what the AI may access and do.
5. Demonstrate a denied action and the stop behavior.
6. Walk through human review, verification and release responsibilities.
7. Practice reporting uncertainty, a bad output and a possible incident.
8. Show where the workbook, evidence and support routes live.
9. Confirm that reporting friction or failure is expected and will not be penalized.
10. Ask each participant to explain their own responsibilities.

## Start decision

The pilot lead records start, delay or stop, the exact scope, unresolved limitations and decision owner. Start only the work and authority approved in the charter.

## Stop here if

Any participant cannot explain the boundary, stop conditions or support route; baseline checks fail; access differs from the tested configuration; or required support is unavailable.

Record completion in the [Pilot Workbook](../reference/pilot-workbook.md). The full sequence is in [start a pilot](../../procedures/pilot/start-pilot.md) and [onboard the team](../../procedures/pilot/onboard-team.md).
