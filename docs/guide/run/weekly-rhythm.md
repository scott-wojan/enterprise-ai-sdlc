# Run the Weekly Operating Rhythm

## Why am I here?

Turn individual work items into shared learning and catch unsafe or unproductive patterns before they spread.

**Owner:** Pilot lead  
**Participants:** Engineering manager, delivery representatives, product, platform, risk, verification, support and measurement owners as needed  
**Time:** 30 minutes each week  
**Bring:** Current workbook, work-item evidence, incidents, support requests and measure updates  
**Leave with:** Continue, restrict, pause or remediate decisions and named actions

## Before the review

The pilot lead collects only decision-relevant evidence. Do not require the team to recreate every prompt or narrate routine work. Surface exceptions, meaningful improvements, failures, rework, control events, team friction and cost.

## Use this agenda

1. Confirm the charter and boundary have not changed.
2. Review work attempted and accepted.
3. Compare material observations with the baseline.
4. Review quality, rework and product acceptance.
5. Review denied actions, exceptions, incidents and evidence gaps.
6. Review support burden and recurring questions.
7. Ask what created confidence, cognitive load, frustration or learning.
8. Examine cost and any effort transferred to reviewers, platform or operations.
9. Identify repeated patterns rather than debating isolated anecdotes.
10. Decide continue unchanged, restrict, pause or remediate.
11. Assign every action an owner and due date.

## Questions that expose hidden cost

- Did speed earlier in the process create more review or rework later?
- Did experienced people absorb work that is not visible in delivery measures?
- Did people accept output they did not fully understand?
- Are the same context gaps or tool failures recurring?
- Are controls preventing problems or merely recording them after the fact?

## Stop or pause when

Quality, security, control integrity, participant safety or recoverability materially deteriorates; a required decision owner is unavailable; the operating boundary changed without approval; or a material incident remains unresolved.

Record the review in the [weekly section of the Pilot Workbook](../reference/pilot-workbook.md). The canonical operating procedure is [operate the pilot](../../procedures/pilot/operate-pilot.md).
