# Is Your Organization Ready to Consider a Pilot?

## Why am I here?

Use this quick screen to decide whether it is reasonable to invest in detailed preparation. This is not the readiness assessment.

**Owner:** Pilot lead  
**Participants:** Engineering manager, platform lead, security or risk partner  
**Time:** 30 minutes  
**Bring:** The proposed business intent and a possible team  
**Leave with:** Proceed to preparation, remediate first, or do not proceed

## Quick screen

Answer each question with **yes**, **not yet**, or **no**.

- Is an executive sponsor willing to own the enterprise decision?
- Is a pilot lead able to coordinate the work and stop it?
- Is there a stable team with a real but non-critical use case?
- Can the organization define what repositories, data and tools may be accessed?
- Can the team run its existing build, test, review and release controls reliably?
- Can access be removed quickly and independently?
- Can the team report failures and uncertainty without pressure to make the pilot look successful?
- Is there enough baseline information to compare delivery, quality, operations and team experience?
- Will security, risk or compliance partners participate before execution starts?
- Will leadership accept a decision to restrict or stop?

## Interpret the result

**Proceed to preparation** when every question is yes or has a small, owned remediation that can be completed before launch.

**Remediate first** when the proposed pilot may be viable but ownership, repository health, access controls, baseline evidence or support is not ready.

**Do not proceed** when accountability is absent, critical controls cannot be enforced, the work is unsuitable for a first pilot, or the result has been predetermined.

Do not total the answers into a maturity score. A single material gap can make the pilot unsafe or uninterpretable.

## Record the decision

Write down:

- the decision;
- the person who made it;
- the reasons;
- every required remediation, owner and due date;
- the date for reassessment.

The full preparation phase uses the canonical [readiness decision procedure](../../procedures/readiness/assess-and-decide.md).

## Stop here if

Any participant identifies a material unknown about authority, data exposure, technical containment, verification or recovery. Assign the unknown and resolve it before selecting tools or launching work.
