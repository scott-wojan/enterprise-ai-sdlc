# Who Must Participate

## Why am I here?

Use this page to make sure the pilot has the people needed to make decisions, operate safely and learn. This is not a committee roster. It is a list of accountabilities that must be covered.

**Owner:** Executive sponsor  
**Participants:** Pilot lead and leaders supplying each required role  
**Time:** 30 to 45 minutes  
**Bring:** The default pilot shape and candidate organization  
**Leave with:** A named owner for every required accountability

## Required accountabilities

### Executive sponsor

Owns why the enterprise is running the pilot, supplies authority and resources, removes organizational blockers, and accepts the final decision. The sponsor does not declare success without evidence.

### Pilot lead

Coordinates preparation and operation, maintains the workbook, convenes decisions, tracks remediation and can pause the pilot. This is the first person to name.

### Engineering manager

Owns the participating team's capacity, delivery health and local operating conditions. Protects the team from pressure to hide friction or bypass controls.

### Delivery team

Performs the work, checks outputs, records material observations and reports uncertainty. Team members remain responsible for work they accept or apply.

### Product or service owner

Confirms that selected work is valuable, supplies acceptance criteria and evaluates product consequences. AI does not decide what should be built.

### Platform or enablement lead

Provides approved tools, environments, access enforcement, logging, support and revocation. Keeps the pilot inside the technical boundary.

### Security, risk and compliance partner

Interprets applicable obligations, challenges unsafe assumptions, approves relevant exceptions and participates in incident decisions.

### Verification and release owners

Confirm that checks are trustworthy, review is independent where required and the ordinary release process remains authoritative.

### Measurement owner

Establishes the baseline, protects measurement integrity, combines quantitative and qualitative evidence, and prevents a single productivity number from deciding expansion.

## Combining roles

A smaller organization may assign multiple accountabilities to one person. Do not combine roles when doing so allows someone to approve their own consequential action, weaken a control they are subject to, or declare their own output successful.

## Confirm participation

For each accountability, record the named person, backup, decisions they own, expected time commitment and escalation path. A title or department is not enough.

## Stop here if

- No sponsor will accept the final decision.
- No one has authority to pause or stop the pilot.
- Security, platform or release participation is deferred until after launch.
- The team is expected to absorb the pilot without capacity or support.
