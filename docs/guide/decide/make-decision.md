# Continue, Restrict, Expand or Stop

## Why am I here?

Make an accountable decision for the exact pilot scope based on evidence, not enthusiasm, fear or a composite score.

**Owner:** Executive sponsor or named enterprise decision owner  
**Participants:** Pilot lead, measurement owner, engineering, product, platform, risk, quality, operations and delivery representatives  
**Time:** 60 minutes  
**Bring:** Completed evidence review, unresolved constraints and remediation status  
**Leave with:** A recorded decision, scope, reasons, restrictions and next decision date

## Choose one decision

### Continue

Keep the same team, use case, operating mode and boundary while gathering more evidence or realizing a demonstrated benefit.

### Restrict

Narrow the work, authority, tools, context, participants or duration because evidence shows value only under tighter conditions or exposes unacceptable uncertainty.

### Expand

Add one bounded capability, use case, team or authority dimension. Expansion requires evidence that the current operating conditions are effective and that the next increment is separately ready. It does not authorize broad rollout.

### Stop

End the pilot or a use case because value is absent, risk or burden is unacceptable, controls are ineffective, conditions cannot be remediated or the organization has learned enough not to proceed.

## Apply non-negotiable constraints

Do not expand when there is material deterioration in quality, security, control integrity, recoverability or participant safety. Do not let speed, usage or generated volume override those constraints.

The most restrictive applicable policy, readiness, risk or control result governs.

## Record the reasoning

State:

- the exact scope of the decision;
- evidence supporting it;
- evidence against it;
- uncertainties and limitations;
- restrictions that remain;
- remediation required;
- people who participated;
- accountable decision owner and date;
- conditions and date for reassessment.

Someone who generated or executed the work cannot be the only person establishing its success.

## Stop here if

The decision owner is unavailable, material evidence is disputed or missing, an incident is unresolved, or participants are being asked to approve expansion outside their authority.

Record the result in the [Pilot Workbook](../reference/pilot-workbook.md). The canonical decision process is [evaluate and decide](../../procedures/pilot/evaluate-and-decide.md).
