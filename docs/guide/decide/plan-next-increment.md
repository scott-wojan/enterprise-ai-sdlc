# Standardize What Worked and Plan the Next Increment

## Why am I here?

Convert the decision into a small set of operating changes without turning one pilot into an uncontrolled enterprise rollout.

**Owner:** Pilot lead  
**Participants:** Sponsor, engineering, product, platform, risk, enablement, support and measurement owners  
**Time:** 60 to 90 minutes  
**Bring:** Final decision, evidence review, remediation and participant feedback  
**Leave with:** Retained practices, retired practices and one bounded next-increment plan

## Standardize selectively

Retain only practices supported by evidence. Candidates may include:

- a suitable use-case definition;
- a reusable context or repository instruction;
- a tested access boundary;
- a verification or review pattern;
- an onboarding exercise;
- a support or incident response;
- a lightweight evidence practice.

Name an owner for each retained practice and place it in the normal enterprise system that will maintain it. The pilot workbook is evidence, not a permanent substitute for operating ownership.

## Remove what did not work

Retire unsafe shortcuts, ineffective prompts, unnecessary reporting, unreliable automation, unused controls and workarounds that depended on exceptional individual effort. Preserve the reason so another team does not repeat the experiment unknowingly.

## Define one next increment

Change one major dimension at a time where practical:

- another similar use case;
- another prepared team;
- a slightly broader context;
- a stronger reusable control;
- Bounded Change for one eligible task after Assisted Work;
- a longer observation period under the same boundary.

For the increment, state the hypothesis, scope, readiness work, control changes, owners, evidence needed, stop conditions and next decision date.

## Reassess, do not inherit

Another team, repository, data class, tool or authority level does not inherit the pilot's approval. Reuse evidence where applicable, but reassess the conditions that changed.

## Close the pilot

Revoke temporary access, archive or link durable evidence, close remediation, communicate the decision to participants and thank people for candid reporting. Publish a plain-language summary that includes limitations and negative findings.

## Stop here if

The proposed next step is described as enterprise-wide, assumes all teams are equivalent, bundles several authority increases, or has no new decision gate.

Use the canonical [decide and standardize](../../procedures/pilot/decide-and-standardize.md) procedure for closure and transfer.
